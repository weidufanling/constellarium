          ABOUT CONSTELLARIUM

          What’s on the globe?

          The globe pins are fictional and show example places. The working specialists are browser tools adapted from Weidu’s own specialist configurations. They run the specific tasks shown on their cards.

          The globe demo and catchment inputs clear when you reload. Saving a working constellation stores its name and tool choices in this browser. Real creator locations require verified opt-in before a pin can appear. Tools can be listed by discipline without a location.
