# Contribute to Constellarium

- **Report a problem.** Include the browser or device, the steps you took, and what happened. Use the example inputs and leave out personal information.
- **Suggest a working specialist.** Describe a specific task people could complete, the inputs it needs, what it returns and any sources or limits. Say whether the method runs in a browser or depends on a service.
- **Check a source.** Include the claim, a public link, and the page or section that supports a correction.

A creator can choose whether to list a place. Keep location or consent details out of public issues. Weidu will verify permission before publishing a geographic pin. Use public material you have permission to share and keep private instructions, attachments and credentials out of submissions.

For proposed interface changes, rebuild the standalone page, check keyboard and touch controls, and describe what you tested. Public wording uses plain language and ordinary punctuation. If the page’s script or styles change, verify the rebuilt security hashes and run the affected controls in a browser.
